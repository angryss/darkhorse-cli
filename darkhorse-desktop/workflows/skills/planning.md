# Skill: Governed Planning

## Authority

Optional OpenSpec input may exist before A1. After A1, `.visu/work/<change-id>/contract.yaml` is sole editable plan authority; proposal/tasks are read-only hash-bound projections.

## Method

1. Collect structural observations without a readiness score or boolean.
2. Before A1, submit one complete draft to the approved materialization adapter.
3. After A1, reject direct projection edits or stale bindings. Amend A1 through the adapter and regenerate.
4. Invoke installed Darkhorse `plan` against the selected project root.
5. Display only fresh governed output. VEP owns readiness, risk tier, and transition truth.
