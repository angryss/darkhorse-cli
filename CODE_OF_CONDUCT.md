# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

DarkHorse contributors are expected to keep discussion professional, constructive, and focused on the work. Healthy disagreement is welcome; harassment, personal attacks, and abusive behavior are not.

Examples of expected behavior:

- Treat contributors with respect.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be clear about assumptions, tradeoffs, and risks.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope and project direction.
- Help keep the project usable for contributors from different environments and backgrounds.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal insults or sustained hostile comments.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Submitting secrets, customer data, or confidential internal materials.
- Disruptive behavior that makes collaboration harder.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, reject contributions, restrict participation, or ban contributors whose behavior violates this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report code of conduct concerns to the project maintainers through the private contact channel listed in `SECURITY.md` once one is established. Until then, open a GitHub issue only if the concern can be discussed publicly without exposing private information.
